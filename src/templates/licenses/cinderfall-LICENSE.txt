Cinderfall -- an example module for the Digidream engine and studio.

Textures and music: Freedoom (https://freedoom.github.io), under its licence below.
  Wall textures are composited from Freedoom's patches as its textures.cfg lays them
  out; flats and skies are Freedoom's own. They are renamed in this module.
  Music (MIDI, imported into DigidreamSynth DAW projects):
    title     "Mists Over the Mire" by Tyler "Picklehammer" P. (Freedoom MAP29)
    railhead  "Not My First Rodeo..." by Lola "BlueWorrior" Harvey (MAP01)
    hub       "Sewers" by Discoholic, remix of "Wasteland Scavengers" by Samuel "Blastfrog" Oliver (MAP12)
    foundry   "Under Maintenance" by Lola "BlueWorrior" Harvey (MAP05)
    mine      "Dark Underworld" by Lola "BlueWorrior" Harvey (MAP02)
    oldtown   "Terrible Secrets" by Lola "BlueWorrior" Harvey (MAP09)
    heart     "Devoured by the Jaws of Defeat" by Korp, based on "Paranoia" by Tyler "Picklehammer" P. (MAP30)
    boss      "The Machine" by Lola "BlueWorrior" Harvey (MAP31)
    victory   "Distance" by Korp (Phase 1 victory)

Fonts (assets/ui/fonts/):
  Special Elite by Astigmatic -- Apache License 2.0 (see SPECIAL_ELITE_LICENSE.txt)
  Bebas Neue by Dharma Type -- SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see BEBAS_NEUE_OFL.txt)
The panel skins (assets/ui/skins/) are cut from the Freedoom textures above.

Everything else -- the levels, the monsters, people, weapons and props (ThreeSprite
projects), the sounds (DigidreamSound), the icons and the graphs -- was made for
this module and is dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0).
